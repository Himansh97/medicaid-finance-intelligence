# Building the dashboard

No dashboard is implemented. This contract supersedes the earlier assumption that the export was safe to sum without coverage and lineage caveats.

## Arrangement view

Use `data/published/sdp_tableau_extract.csv`, generated by `python -m src.sdp.tableau_export`. It contains 1,061 candidate arrangement groups; 329 have no resolved usable amount. Eight archive rows with incomplete rating dates cannot be assigned to a year and remain in the archive.

Filter to one `rating_period_year`, meaning the START year of the rating period. This is not a calendar-year spending allocation. For 2024 there are 285 groups and a known projected subtotal of $92.71B. Show the number of known and unknown amounts beside the subtotal. Never replace null amounts with zero or label a partial subtotal as total state spending.

`lineage_status` distinguishes `SINGLE_FILING`, `INFERRED_FROM_IDENTIFIER` and `AMBIGUOUS`. Numeric amendments sort numerically. Tied versions and unknown review types are unresolved; suffix-distinguished arrangements remain separate. Filename inference is not proof that every arrangement family or legal supersession is correct. `candidate_identifiers` lists the underlying archive rows for source review.

`amount_status` distinguishes `KNOWN`, `MISSING_LATEST_AMOUNT` and `UNRESOLVED_LINEAGE`. A newer missing amount must never fall back to an older known amount. Genuine reported zeros remain zero and carry `amount_is_zero`; exclude zeros only if the visual explicitly describes that choice.

## State cap view

Use `data/published/sdp_state_caps.csv` as a separate state-grain table. `cap_document_sum_usd` sums the archive's stated document ceilings, with `cap_document_count` and `source_identifiers`. It is not deduplicated statutory exposure. Overlapping determinations still need source review.

Do not physically join this table onto arrangement rows. Use a separate sheet/table or a BI relationship with state-level aggregation. Never add document caps to projected payment amounts. The arrangement extract deliberately has no repeated cap measure.

## Proposed visuals

1. Archive coverage: 823 of 1,157 documents carry a usable amount; 334 do not. Use the archive for these counts, not the version-selected extract. Title: “29% of listed documents have no usable extracted amount.”
2. State bars: known projected amounts for the selected start year, with missing-count labels and source links. California has the largest known 2024 subtotal, not Texas. Do not claim complete rankings.
3. Reporting proxy: 2020 supplemental-payment assessment beside the selected year's projected subtotal. The preserved assessment snapshot is not current SDP reporting quality. For 2024, two of the ten largest known subtotals have Low concern assessments.

## Required visible caveats

- Projected amounts, not actual spending; selected rating-period start year shown.
- Partial coverage: show known and unknown arrangement counts.
- Identifier-inferred lineage is not source-verified supersession.
- Supplemental payment reporting in 2020 is a proxy, not a current SDP assessment.
- Caps are document subtotals with unresolved overlap, not additional projected payments.
- No enrollment denominator exists: no PMPM/per-capita figure can be supported.

Keep source URL, source hash, retrieval timestamp and candidate identifiers accessible. Federal shares come from the selected document's form; they may not reconcile to an approval-letter fallback total and must not be shown as a validated split without further review.
